05 / FEASIBILITY & VIABILITY
FEASIBILITY
  • Uses existing GIS, DEM, satellite & hydrological technologies.
  • Modular, scalable and cost-effective using open-source tools.
  • Can provide real-time flood-risk visualization and decision support.
CHALLENGES
  • Limited or poor-quality geographical and rainfall data.
  • High computational requirements for accurate simulations.
  • Uncertainty in flood predictions and real-world conditions.
STRATEGIES
  • Reliable datasets with data preprocessing and validation.
  • Cloud/HPC computing and optimized simulations.
  • Multiple scenarios validated with historical flood events.
